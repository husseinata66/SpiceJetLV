## 5. Limitations

- The Integration Layer's outgoing request to Galileo and Galileo's raw answer to it are not observable (BND-03). No case claims they are the same or different.
- Payloads are reported by the platform back end, not captured on the network (BND-04).
- Availability is live: a flight present on one side only may reflect cache vs live-poll timing. That is recorded (`availabilitySource`) but not established as a mechanism, so such flights stay Confirmed under the approved rule.
- Execution used a throwaway Playwright control script (scratchpad), not the MCP/BrowserDriver layer.
